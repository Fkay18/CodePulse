About CodePulse

Learning in public, one article at a time.

CodePulse is a place for practical lessons on Python, Django, backend systems, and the kind of problem-solving that makes software development feel real.

It is built around the idea that great engineering usually starts with curiosity, careful thinking, and a willingness to learn out loud. This blog captures that process: the experiments, the breakdowns, the fixes, and the wins.

Why it exists

Built for curiosity, clarity, and better systems.

CodePulse exists to make software learning feel useful instead of theoretical. The focus is on small, repeatable lessons: understanding how things work, improving the process, and applying knowledge in real projects.

Whether it is an API design decision, a database issue, or a Django pattern, each article is meant to help the reader build with more confidence.
